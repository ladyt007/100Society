Friday, September 18, 2026

100 Women of Excellence Recognition Ceremony

Held at the United States Capitol in Washington, DC, this ceremony honors 100 women for leadership, professional achievement, community service, and impact. Each honoree is individually recognized and pinned before the room.

This is also the day 100 Society officially opens for enrollment — the accountability community built to carry that recognition forward all year.

  • Recognition ceremony at the U.S. Capitol
  • 100 Women of Excellence Brunch immediately following
  • Live enrollment opens for 100 Society membership
