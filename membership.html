Common questions

Before you join

What happens right after I join?

You'll complete a short intake assessment. It's how the team learns about your business and matches you with an accountability partner — you'll also get an evaluation back with guidance specific to what you're building.

Is the $1,000 one-on-one available if I pay monthly?

The complimentary 1:1 session with Erica is reserved for members who pay in full for the year. Monthly members still get full access to breakout groups, Wisdom Wednesday, and every other membership benefit.

Where does membership actually live?

Community, scheduling, and content all live on our membership platform. This site is where you'll find everything you need to know before joining — the link above takes you to checkout.

Can I bring a nomination for someone else?

Yes. The Society keeps a running list of national award opportunities, and members are encouraged to nominate one another.
