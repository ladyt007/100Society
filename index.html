A home for women who hold each other to the standard

Your accountability partner, your tribe, your access.

100 Women Society is a year-long membership for women entrepreneurs and leaders — built on accountability, weekly exposure, and a network that opens doors for one another.

Join the Society See Membership Details
Why it exists

Ambition is easier to sustain in company.

Most women in business are surrounded by admirers, not accountability. 100 Society pairs you with a partner who checks in on your goals, a weekly room of women building alongside you, and a founder who puts her own network behind your next move.

How membership works

Three pillars, every month

I. Advise

Guidance built around you

An intake assessment matches you with an accountability partner, and monthly breakout groups of ten put you in a room with Erica to work through what's next for your business.

II. Share

Your news, in front of the room

Post your next event or launch and the team builds the content to promote it across the Society's network — with members showing up to support you in return.

III. Promote

Recognition that compounds

After your first month, you're eligible for nomination toward national recognitions, with Erica personally writing your recommendation letter.

Every week, every month

A rhythm you can build a business around

  • Wisdom Wednesday — a live weekly session with a rotating guest leader chosen for what the room is working on
  • Share day — your event or milestone turned into a carousel and reshared to the full Society network
  • Monthly "how-to" training on getting featured and covered by media
  • Monthly breakout groups with Erica, in pods of ten
  • A curated calendar of member events and speaking opportunities, with first notice to the Society
